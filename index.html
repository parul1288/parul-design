About

Parul Tater.
Designer who builds,
builder who designs.

I spent eight years at the intersection of design and engineering — as a frontend developer who cared about how things felt to use, and as a UX developer who could implement what she designed. At Cloudera and Alectio, I owned work end to end: from design discussions through to shipping and validation.

I'm returning to tech with a clearer sense of what I want: design products that are well thought through and well built. I learn by making things real.
